How can we predict an employees Days Away from their job based on an injury?

lm(formula = log1p(dafw_num_away) ~ factor(Size) + factor(establishment_type), 
    data = data)

Holding establishment size category constant, establishments classified as type 2 have an estimated 0.404 higher log(1 + days away) than establishments classified as type 1 (95% CI: 0.380 to 0.429). The model explains only approximately 0.35% of the variation in log-transformed days away from work (R² = 0.0035), suggesting that establishment size and type alone are insufficient to explain differences in lost workdays.

Our Prompt:

We are working on a STAT 330 project looking at whether the size and type of a workplace establishment have a relationship with the number of days employees miss work due to injuries or illnesses.

Our response variable is dafw_num_away, and our main predictors are establishment size and establishment type. We also plan to look at incident type, state, average number of employees, and total hours worked.

For our original analysis, we used multiple linear regression with a log transformation on days away from work. Our R-squared was 0.003475, which shows that our model explains very little of the variation in days away from work. One thing we noticed was that establishment type 2 had a higher estimated coefficient than type 1, and there seemed to be different patterns across establishment sizes and types.

We want to try using a random forest model to see if it can find patterns in our data that our original regression might have missed.

Can you help us figure out how to create a random forest model in R using our dataset? We would also like to compare it with our original regression model using repeated cross-validation and RMSE. Explain how to interpret the results and suggest a way to show the comparison in our final project. Please don’t make up any results since we want to use our actual data.
